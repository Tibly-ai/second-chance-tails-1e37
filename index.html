Who we are

A small rescue with a very short list of rules.

Second Chance Tails Rescue works with county shelters across northeast Iowa that have more animals than kennels. When a euthanasia list goes out, we answer it. Animals ride back with us, get seen by our vet partner within 48 hours, and go straight into a foster home instead of another cage.

Foster families tell us the things a kennel card never could: that he sleeps under the coffee table, that she is fine with the cat but not with the vacuum, that he needs a fenced yard and a person who is home by five. That is what makes an adoption stick.

The route home

From a shelter list to a family, usually in under six weeks.

Nothing here is complicated. It just has to happen fast, and someone has to answer the phone.

  1. 01

    The call comes in

    A shelter manager sends us names and a deadline. We confirm what we can take that week based on open foster beds, never more.

  2. 02

    Vet first, always

    Exam, vaccines, spay or neuter, microchip, and treatment for whatever the shelter did not have budget to catch. Medical records travel with the animal.

  3. 03

    Settled with a foster

    Two weeks of house living tells us more than two months of kennel notes. Fosters write the profile you read on the adopt page.

  4. 04

    Meet, match, go home

    You meet in the foster's living room or at our adoption lounge. If it clicks, the adoption is finished that day and we check in at week one and month three.
